# Output Style and Evaluation Guidelines

## Core Principles
1. **Dumb Data Isolation:** The AI models only evaluate and structure qualitative judgements. Scores, rankings, and mathematical weights are computed deterministically in application code.
2. **Neutral Tone:** Assessments must be factual, professional, and clear. Avoid overly enthusiastic or colloquial prose.
3. **Language Consistency:**
   - In Vietnamese context, notes and descriptions should be written in natural Vietnamese technical terminology.
   - In English context, write in clean, concise English.
4. **Data Privacy Safety:** Never copy or echo sensitive candidate personally identifiable information (PII) such as phone numbers, citizen IDs, or street addresses into notes or outputs.
