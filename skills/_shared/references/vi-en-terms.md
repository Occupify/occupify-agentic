# Vietnamese - English Freelance and Technical Terms Reference

## Freelancing & Employment Types
- Toàn thời gian / Full-time: 40 hours per week, standard engagement.
- Bán thời gian / Part-time: typically 20-30 hours per week or flexible schedule.
- Dự án / Fixed-price / Freelance: deliverables-based contracting.
- Lương tháng / Monthly budget: fixed recurring monthly compensation.
- Lương giờ / Hourly rate: per-hour compensation billing.

## Seniority & Experience Levels
- Mới đi làm / Thực tập sinh / Entry level: < 1-2 years experience, foundational knowledge.
- Trung cấp / Intermediate: 2-5 years experience, independent task execution.
- Chuyên gia / Senior / Expert: 5+ years experience, system design, architectural leadership.

## Common Domain Terms
- Thương mại điện tử / E-commerce: online retail, order processing, checkout, payments.
- Công nghệ tài chính / Fintech: banking, payment gateways, electronic wallets, core banking.
- Y tế số / HealthTech: medical record management, telemedicine, doctor booking.
- Giáo dục số / EdTech: online learning platforms, course LMS, video streaming.
- Logistics / Chuỗi cung ứng: freight forwarding, warehouse inventory, order tracking.
